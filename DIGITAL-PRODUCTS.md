## Service-business pricing and profit collection

Four related Excel products share a pricing and completed-job foundation, with different workflows:

| Product | Distinct workflow | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cleaning | Recurring visits, per-visit discounts, and agreement profit | Draft workbook product |
| Mobile detailing | Extra service hours, supplies, and add-on pricing | Draft workbook product |
| Lawn care | Seasonal visits and additional equipment costs | Draft workbook product |
| Freelance | Revision rounds, revision hours, and project pricing | Draft workbook product |

The collection includes blank templates and fictional examples, target-margin pricing, payment-fee allowances, actual-job profit tracking for 200 jobs, input checks, and protected formula cells. Scenario checks and independent recalculation of exported files are documented in the project workspace. These are alternatives for different owners, not four separate SaaS systems. Buyer files are not distributed through this repository.
