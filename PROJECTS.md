## Applications

### Skalet

- **Purpose:** measure PDF construction plans on iPad and iPhone and preserve measurement context for review and sharing.
- **Work:** PDF import, named scales, calibration, snapping, saved lengths and areas, Undo/Redo, PDF annotation/export, and trade-specific takeoff calculations.
- **Recent development:** roofing surface calculations and squares, siding net quantities, concrete quantities, and a shared totals view for a sheet or whole plan. Work also includes source-sheet navigation and label readability improvements.
- **Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, and PDFKit.
- **Status:** in development; not publicly released. Development builds and calculation/export checks are documented; current on-device smoothness and installation of the latest build still require testing.
- **Roof Lab:** a separate experimental build used to explore roof selection and trade workflows. It is part of Skalet development, not a separate released product.
- **Link:** [Case study](https://garvindigital.com/projects/skalet).

### Hearth

- **Purpose:** connect B&B guest check-in and meal choices with front-desk and kitchen preparation.
- **Work:** guest verification, breakfast selections and serving times, dietary preferences, kitchen states/totals, menu management, multilingual guest content, manual bookings, reviewed CSV import, and separate extras orders/payment-recording workflows.
- **Technology:** React, TypeScript, Fastify, embedded PostgreSQL/PGlite, and a separate SwiftUI/Core Data iPad project.
- **Status:** local development and private pilot preparation with fictional demo data. Little Hotelier synchronization, hosted production operation, and full native/offline acceptance remain unfinished. Recording payment received is not card processing.
- **Link:** [Case study](https://garvindigital.com/projects/hearth). The website case study may summarize an earlier prototype stage.

### JARVIS

- **Purpose:** explore a local assistant for business records, briefings, and supervised drafting.
- **Work:** persistent records, conversational dashboard, drafting queue, pause/retry/cancel controls, owner review, saved preferences, and optional local model and speech integrations.
- **Technology:** Python, SQLite, browser JavaScript, Ollama, and optional local speech tooling.
- **Status:** local prototype. Draft acceptance does not publish content or execute actions in external business accounts; always-on autonomous business operation remains a broader goal.

### AI receptionist and calendar integration

- **Purpose:** explore an authenticated SaaS dashboard and calendar connections.
- **Work represented in the saved workspace:** React/TypeScript interface, account and recovery screens, dashboard navigation, and calendar integration UI/API wiring.
- **Technology:** React, TypeScript, Vite, and Supabase integration code.
- **Status:** development prototype. The presence of integration code does not establish working Outlook/Google connections, live receptionist functionality, or a production deployment. Current runtime behavior has not been revalidated for this portfolio update.
